

This commit updates the **CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md** from Contributor Covenant v1.4 to v2.1 for the VSCodium project. Here are the key changes:

### Major Updates:

1. **Our Pledge (lines 5-13)**: Expanded and more inclusive language
   - Added "visible or invisible disability," "sex characteristics," "caste," and "color"
   - Added commitment to creating "diverse, inclusive, and healthy community"

2. **Our Standards (lines 17-37)**: Updated behavioral expectations
   - Added emphasis on "empathy and kindness"
   - Expanded on "constructive feedback" and personal responsibility
   - Added more specific examples of unacceptable behavior regarding sexual attention and harassment

3. **Enforcement Responsibilities (renamed from "Our Responsibilities", lines 39-49)**: Clarified roles
   - Changed from "project maintainers" to "community leaders"
   - Added explicit mention of communicating moderation decisions

4. **Enforcement Guidelines (NEW section, lines 69-113)**: Added detailed response levels
   - **1. Correction**: Private warning
   - **2. Warning**: Restricted interaction with temporary/permanent ban potential
   - **3. Temporary Ban**: Time-limited communication ban
   - **4. Permanent Ban**: Permanent removal from community spaces

5. **Attribution (lines 117-123)**: Updated references
   - Now points to v2.1 instead of v1.4
   - Updated URLs to current Contributor Covenant website

**Total changes**: +98 lines, -21 lines (mostly restructuring and expansion with more detailed guidelines)
